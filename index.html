For Parents · Occupational Therapy · California

When your child's world feels too loud, too fast, or too far away.

Some children live in bodies that feel confusing or unsafe. Sound can feel sharp. Stillness can feel impossible. Big feelings can arrive without warning. Connection can feel risky. Sensorium Therapy offers neuro-focused occupational therapy that works with your child's nervous system, not against it, through sensory integration, emotional safety, attuned relationships, and play.

Integrated Nervous System Development

We help children whose sensory, emotional, and relational systems feel scattered. Through play-based, body-aware occupational therapy rooted in attachment and early infant–parent mental health principles, we help the nervous system read and respond to the world more clearly.

What We Address

  • Sensory sensitivity and sensory seeking
  • Attention, focus, and motor coordination
  • Emotional regulation and big feelings
  • Listening and auditory processing
  • Attachment and relational confidence

DIR Floortime® Work

As a certified DIR Floortime® Expert, Sanette meets your child where they are. She follows their interests, joins their play, and builds developmental capacities through warm, attuned interaction. Relational safety first. Everything else grows from there.

Interactive Metronome® & Tomatis® Listening

Alongside relational and body-based work, Sanette uses Interactive Metronome® for rhythm, timing, and motor planning, and Tomatis®, a specialized listening program that delivers gentle sound through both the ears and the bones of the head. This kind of listening helps the brain organize sound, balance, and body signals more effectively, supporting calmer arousal, better focus, and a clearer sense of what the body is feeling on the inside.

It starts with parents.

You are the most important regulator in your child's life. Every plan includes practical, brain-based coaching for grown-ups, so the safety your child finds in session can come home with them.

About

Sanette Louwrens, OTR/L

Sanette is an occupational therapist who helps children and families move from chronic survival mode toward a more regulated, connected way of living. Her work connects sensory processing, emotion regulation, relational safety, and symbolic capacity, because the nervous system never works in pieces.

  • OTR/L — NBCOT® #994802 · CBOT #10729
  • DIR Floortime® Expert — listed in the official DIR® directory
  • Tomatis® Level 4 Consultant — neuro-auditory listening and bone conduction
  • Interactive Metronome® certified
  • SIPT / EASI — sensory integration assessment
  • Circle of Security Parenting™ facilitator
  • NAPA / UC Davis Infant–Parent Mental Health Program alumna, IPMH, 2021–2022
  • NMT Phase 1 trained — Neurosequential Model of Therapeutics
